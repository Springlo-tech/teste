Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install an app to use Augmented Reality? +

No. The experience runs directly through the mobile browser using WebAR technology.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android? +

Yes. The experience is fully compatible with major Android and iPhone devices.

Are there any extra costs besides the development fee? +

Not necessarily. We create an exclusive account for you on the Cloudflare Pages platform and upload the entire project there, ensuring a high-performance infrastructure and complete autonomy for your page.

If you want a custom domain (e.g., www.yourbook.com), you just need to purchase it separately, which costs around $10 to $15 per year.

For most authors and publishers, this dedicated structure is more than enough to host the files securely and handle all regular traffic.

How long does the development process take? +

Timeline varies depending on project complexity, typically taking between 5 and 15 business days.

Is the landing page hosted permanently? +

Yes. We handle the deployment and deliver everything fully functional, ready for promotion.
